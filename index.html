About

Practical leadership.
Disciplined execution.

I connect design intent, field conditions, subcontractor performance and schedule priorities so teams can make clear decisions and keep work moving safely.

“A superintendent’s job is to create the conditions for every trade to succeed—safely, efficiently and in the right sequence.”

Marwan Diab

My experience spans greenfield construction, occupied-site work, active rail corridors, advanced food-processing facilities, higher-education projects, hospitals, aircraft hangars, municipal infrastructure and complex structural-steel installations.

I work closely with project managers during pre-construction on constructability, logistics, sequencing and schedule development. In the field, I lead daily coordination, safety expectations, quality control, short-interval planning, look-ahead scheduling and issue resolution.

My approach is direct and collaborative: understand the work, remove constraints early, communicate clearly and hold the team accountable to the plan.

Leadership Strengths

What I bring
to a project.

Strong field leadership is a balance of preparation, communication, technical understanding and consistent follow-through.

01

Pre-construction input

Constructability reviews, logistics planning, sequencing, site access strategy, schedule development and early risk identification.

02

Schedule control

Short-interval plans, three-week look-aheads, constraint tracking, recovery planning and clear communication of priorities.

03

Trade coordination

Daily alignment of crews, equipment, deliveries, inspections, temporary works and interfaces between disciplines.

04

Safety culture

Visible leadership, direct expectations, proactive hazard control and consistent accountability across the site team.

05

Quality execution

Mock-ups, inspections, deficiency prevention, documentation and early resolution of design or installation conflicts.

Stakeholder communication

Clear reporting and practical problem-solving with owners, consultants, inspectors, utilities, subcontractors and project teams.
